    About

    Schedules from the open GTFS feeds of TTC, GO Transit, MiWay, Brampton Transit, YRT, Durham Region Transit, HSR, Oakville Transit and Burlington Transit. Routing runs in your browser (Connection Scan Algorithm) with walking transfers up to 400 m. Live delays come from the agencies' GTFS-Realtime feeds, refreshed about every 10 minutes. Walking is straight-line × 1.3 at 4.7 km/h; fares are ignored.
